How to Record Calls on Android 13 Without Root

CallVault records your calls on Android 13 with an accessibility service. No root.

Steps for Android 13

  1. Install the CallVault APK from the releases page.
  2. Open the app. Tap Grant permissions.
  3. Android 13 blocks the accessibility service for a sideloaded app. To fix this, open the app info page, tap the 3 dots, and tap Allow restricted settings.
  4. Open Accessibility settings. Turn on CallVault.
  5. Make or take a call. CallVault records it automatically.

Record both sides on Android 13

Turn on the speaker option in the app. The microphone then picks up the other person. The result depends on your phone model.
