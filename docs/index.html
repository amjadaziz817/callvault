Why CallVault

  • Automatic. It records every call with no extra tap.
  • No root. It uses an Android accessibility service.
  • Both sides. It can turn on the speaker to capture the other person.
  • Private. The files stay on your phone. There is no cloud and no account.
  • Open source. You can read and build the full code.
  • Free. There is no ad and no payment.

How to record a call on Android without root

  1. Install the CallVault APK from the releases page.
  2. Grant the microphone and phone permissions.
  3. Turn on the CallVault accessibility service.
  4. Make or take a call. CallVault records it automatically.
  5. Open the app to play, share, or delete a recording.

Frequently asked questions

Can I record calls on Android 14 without root?

Yes. CallVault uses an accessibility service, so it can record on Android 14 without root. Test the voice quality on your phone first.

Why is CallVault not on Google Play?

Google Play bans call recorder apps that use the accessibility method. So you install CallVault from GitHub or F-Droid instead.

Does it record the other person?

On many phones, yes. CallVault can turn on the speaker so the microphone captures the other person. The result depends on your phone model.

Is call recording legal?

The law differs by country. In some places you must tell the other person. Check your local law before you record a call.
