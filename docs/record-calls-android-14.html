How to Record Calls on Android 14 Without Root

Android 14 blocks normal call recorder apps. CallVault records your calls on Android 14 with an accessibility service. No root.

Why Android 14 is different

On Android 14, Google blocks the microphone during a call for a normal app. Google also blocks the accessibility service for a sideloaded app by default. CallVault uses the accessibility method, so you must allow it once.

Steps for Android 14

  1. Install the CallVault APK from the releases page.
  2. Open the app. Tap Grant permissions.
  3. Open the app info page. Tap the 3 dots. Tap Allow restricted settings.
  4. Open Accessibility settings. Turn on CallVault.
  5. Make or take a call. CallVault records it automatically.

Record both sides on Android 14

Turn on the speaker option in the app. The microphone then picks up the other person. The result depends on your phone model. Test it with 1 call first.
