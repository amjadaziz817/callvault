How to Record Calls on Android Without Root

This guide shows how to record phone calls on Android without root. It works on Android 10 to Android 14.

What you need

  • An Android phone with Android 8.0 or later.
  • The CallVault app. It is free and open source.

Step 1: Install CallVault

Download the APK from the releases page. Open the file and allow install from unknown sources.

Step 2: Grant the permissions

Open CallVault. Tap Grant permissions. Allow the microphone, the phone, and the notification access.

Step 3: Turn on the accessibility service

Tap Open accessibility settings. Find CallVault in the list. Turn it on.

On Android 13 and later, Android blocks the service for a sideloaded app. To fix this, open the app info page, tap the 3 dots, and tap Allow restricted settings. Then turn on the service.

Step 4: Record a call

Make or take a call. CallVault records it automatically. Open the app to see the recording.

How to record both sides of the call

Without root, Android does not give a clean copy of the other person's voice. CallVault can turn on the speaker during the call. The microphone then picks up the other person. Keep the speaker option on in the app. The result depends on your phone model, so test it first.

Why not Google Play?

Google Play bans call recorder apps that use the accessibility method. So you install CallVault from GitHub or F-Droid. This is safe, because the full source code is open.

Is it legal to record a call?

The law differs by country. In many places you may record a call that you take part in. In some places you must tell the other person. A secret recording can lead to a dispute. Check your local law before you record.
