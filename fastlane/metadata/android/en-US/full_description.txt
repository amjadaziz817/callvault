CallVault records your phone calls automatically. It works without root. It is
free and open source.

CallVault uses an Android accessibility service to record a call. So it records
on Android 10 and later, where normal apps cannot. Your recordings stay on your
phone in internal storage. There is no account and no server.

Features:
* Records every call automatically.
* Works without root.
* Records both sides when the phone allows it.
* Saves each recording with the phone number and the time.
* Plays, shares, and deletes a recording.
* Logs a deal and sends a WhatsApp confirmation.
* Keeps the data on your phone. No cloud and no backup.

The recording quality of the other person depends on your phone model. On some
phones CallVault records both voices clearly. On other phones it records only
your voice unless the speaker is on. Test on your phone first.

Legal notice: The law on call recording differs by country. In some places you
must tell the other person. Check your local law before you record a call.
